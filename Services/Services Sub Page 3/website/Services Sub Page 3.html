Infrastructure & Midstream Services

Leano Energy delivers comprehensive infrastructure and midstream services designed to support safe, efficient, and compliant fuel storage and distribution. From initial design and regulatory approvals to installation, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance, we provide fully integrated systems tailored to each client's operational requirements.

Storage Tank & Pump Installation

We design, supply, and install both above-ground and underground fuel storage systems, engineered for durability, safety, and operational efficiency.

OUR SERVICES INCLUDE:

Above-Ground Storage Tanks (ASTs)

Ideal for diesel (50ppm) and Heavy Fuel Oil applications in mining, logistics depots, and industrial facilities. These tanks allow for easy access, maintenance, and high-volume storage.

Underground Storage Tanks (USTs)

Commonly used for petrol (93 & 95 ULP) and aviation fuels where space optimisation, safety, and environmental considerations are critical, such as service stations and airport facilities.

Custom Tank Configurations

Designed to meet specific capacity, site, and operational requirements, including multi-tank systems for large-scale operations.

Pump Sets & Dispensing Systems

Integrated with storage tanks to enable accurate, efficient, and controlled fuel dispensing across fleets and facilities.

All installations are completed in accordance with industry regulations and safety standards, ensuring long term reliability and compliance.
